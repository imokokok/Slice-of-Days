- The source-language fallback is explicitly `zh_CN`. Chinese native Controls no longer silently fall back to the added English catalog. English labels and rules for the new chess UI were corrected and shortened where needed.

## Reproduction

From the project directory, substitute your installed Godot executable for `$godot`:

```powershell
& $godot --headless --path . --editor --quit
& $godot --path . --script tests/integration/test_chess_presentation.gd -- --isolated-save
& $godot --path . --script tests/integration/test_chess_presentation.gd -- --isolated-save --english
& $godot --path . --script tests/integration/test_five_day_flow.gd -- --isolated-save
& $godot --headless --path . --script tests/integration/test_five_day_flow.gd -- --isolated-save --reload-only
& $godot --path . --script tests/integration/test_home_interiors.gd -- --isolated-save
& $godot --path . --script tests/integration/test_ui_review.gd -- --isolated-save
& $godot --headless --path . --script tests/integration/test_ui_review.gd -- --isolated-save --reload-only
```

Do not run these save-writing integration scripts concurrently. Desktop captures and logs are generated into ignored `.runtime/` directories. `test_chess_presentation.gd` captures the selection, all three boards, rule sheets, story and teaching notebook in both languages. `test_home_interiors.gd` captures both painted interiors.

Fresh desktop verification also opened the ordinary `--live-preview` journey, checked its real map and route controls, and inspected the chess host in a maximized native window. For an interactive chess review without altering a player's journey, run:

```powershell
& $godot --path . --script tools/preview_chess_table.gd -- --isolated-save
```

This preview explicitly starts an isolated B/Day 4 fixture and routes through the production module host. It is not a separate mockup or a replacement for testing the five-day progression.

## Visual and functional limits

The recorder's live picture is the actual scene plus a signal-driven waveform/ink response, not semantic AI image generation. Live cloud teaching requires a separately configured service; this review exercised the local game and lesson UI, not a remote model. The current authored four-frame walking asset is retained. Existing nebula observation remains a view of sourced imagery, not a claim to reconstruct unobserved physical 3D structure.
